## What the realizer does with them after the merge

- `fewer` and `same` are separate query kinds on purpose: W2's `_compared`
  reads a `more` query as "the one with more" from the proof, so a fewer
  question sent as `more` was composed as "Tove has more plums." (a wrong
  answer to "who has fewer"). Under their own keys `_compared` returns `{}` and
  the engine's sentence is passed through: correct, but not composed.
- The tie answer of a `more` question is also passed through (`_compared`
  returns `{}` for equal values).
- An order-in-time answer is realized as a plain count or location answer; the
  time it refers to is not said.
