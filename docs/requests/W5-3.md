## Replies composed from a misread meaning

2. **A name that begins with the first person.** `dev_ko_05#3`, `#4`: the pack reads `나은 사탕은
   7개다.` as `[나 사탕, count, 7]` and `나은이 사탕 지금 몇 개야?` as a question about `나 사탕`. Round 5
   says `#3` with the existence verb (`나은은 7개 있습니다`, read back correctly), but `#4` is answered
   about the user (`이 대화에서 가지신 사탕에 대한 말은 나온 적이 없습니다.`). Asked: a declared name is
   never split into the first person and a particle.
3. **A one-syllable name before 은/는 is not split.** `윤은 무화과가 3개 있다.` → `[윤은 무화과, …]`,
   `펠은 …` likewise (`민수는 …` is read). The realizer falls back to the juxtaposed form (`윤 무화과는
   3개`); the user's own `윤은 …` statements would be recorded under `윤은`.
4. **`제가 맡고 있어요` read as a place.** `dev4_ko_b_44#1` `현재 메달 열다섯 개는 제가 맡고 있어요.` and
   `dev4_ko_c_12#2` are recorded as `[나 맡고 메달, count, …]` with `holders: {"나 맡고": {"kind":
   "place"}}`, so the reply is `나 맡고에는 메달이 15개 있습니다.` Asked: `맡고 있다`, `보관하고 있다`,
   `들고 있다` as having (the user holds them), not as a place.
5. **Two holders asked as one subject.** `dev4_ko_c_06#8` `저와 하은이는 지금 스티커가 몇 장 있습니까?`
   is held `not_stated` with `subject: "나와 하은 스티커"`, so the reply names `나와 하은`. Asked: the
   total or the two counts, or `subjects` with both holders.
6. **Titled and relational holders are not declared.** Request W3-1 item 1 asked for
   `holders: {key: {"kind": "named", "said": …}}`; no meaning block carries `named` yet. So
   `dev4_en_c_31#7` says `from Davis to Omar` (the user said `Ms. Davis`), `Now Davis has 11 rulers`,
   and a two-word key is split at its first word: `dev4_ko_c_33#9` `곽 과장` + `나` → `곽과 합쳐서
   15개 있으십니다.` (the thing is dropped because `곽 과장 장작` and `나 장작` look like two things).
   Asked: the `named` entry for every titled or relational holder key a meaning uses, with the
   user's own words in `said`; a key of several words then owns its compound subject (the realizer
   already splits by declared keys, longest first).
7. **The recipient correction stores an ungrammatical sentence.** `_correct_recipient` rewrites the
   earlier statement in place: `dev4_ko_b_43#8` → `시은한테 씨한테는 제가 음료수 다섯 병을 보냈어요.`,
   `dev4_ko_c_50#6` → `나가 선우씨에게서 스티커 한 장을 받았어요.` Round 5 no longer quotes the rewrite
   (it names the two receivers, request G4-1), but the rewrite is the observation later turns
   replay and the explanation quotes. Asked: the title and the particle of the replaced holder
   carried over (`시은한테는`), the user's own form (`제가`), or the revision recorded as a
   structured change of receiver rather than a rewritten sentence.
