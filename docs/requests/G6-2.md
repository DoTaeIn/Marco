# G6-2: why with a restated fact — the fields the reader now gives, and two plans it has no words for

From: goal G6 (understanding round 6), class 13 / item G6.4, branch `r6-why-fact`.
For: goal W6 (realizer round 6), item W6.3, which owns `marco/language/` and the plans. G6 changed
none of it. (G6-1 is kept for the gate gap of G6.0b.)

## What the reader does now

A why that names a holder and/or a number (`Why does Nora have 4?`, `What is the reason Nora has 4?`,
`Why is that number 4?`, `Why do I only have 4?`, `왜 보라가 4개야?`, `왜 4개예요?`,
`보라의 국자가 4개가 된 까닭은 무엇인가?`, `보라 국자가 왜 4개가 되었습니까?`, `왜 나는 4개밖에 없어?`) is read
as `why_count` with an optional `value` (`수량이유물음.facts` in both packs) and resolved in
`ReasoningContext._explain_count` against the current state. The results, and the fields each carries:

1. **Explained** (the number is the holder's current count): the existing explain meaning (`act:
   explain`, `kind: answer`, or `kind: chain` with the ledger on and the holder the last answer's),
   plus `about: {"holder": "보라 국자", "value": 4}`. `holder` is the state's key (holder and thing,
   the user as `나 …` / `I …`); `value` the count, an integer. Today the plan says the whole last
   explanation and ignores `about`.
2. **The restated number is not the count**: `act: hold`, `reason: unresolved`, `differs: true`,
   `about: {"holder": …, "value": <the number said>, "recorded": <the current count>}`. Routed to
   the nearest existing plan, which says *"The conditions in this conversation do not decide the
   answer."* / *"이 대화에서 나온 조건으로는 답을 정하지 못합니다."*. That is safe (it confirms nothing)
   but not true: the count is decided, it is a different number.
3. **Which holder**: `act: ask`, `reason: which_referent`, `word`, `candidates` (state keys). With no
   holder named and a last answer that named none or several, `word` is the number as said
   (`"4"`), because no word pointed at anyone; the plan then says *"I do not know who '4' refers to"*.

## Asked

1. A plan for `{"act": "hold", "reason": "unresolved", "fields_has": ["about", "differs"]}` (or a
   reason of your naming; tell G6 and it will emit it) that says the recorded count of the holder
   and that the number said is not it, without confirming the number said: e.g. *"Nora has 6 pens,
   not 4."* / *"보라 국자는 4개가 아니라 6개입니다."*.
2. W6.3: the explain plans say the holder's count first when the meaning carries `about`
   (*"Nora has 6 pens: …"*), then the derivation as today.
3. `which_referent` with a `word` that is a number: *"Which holder's 4 do you mean: 'Nora pens' or
   'Bo pens'?"*, or tell G6 which field to send instead of `word`.
