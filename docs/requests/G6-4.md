# G6-4: three holds the reader now gives whose words say something else

From: goal G6, reading round 2, branch `g6-reading-2`.
For: the owner of the reply-return sites and `marco/language/` (the realizer round). The reader changed no
reply site and no `meaning` block; each case below returns the closest existing meaning.

1. **A transfer to a holder counted under another key** (`reasoning_context._shaken_by_unread`, the part after
   the unread loop). `가윤 삼촌 민혁은 트럭에 자두를 7개 싣고 있어` is recorded as the count of `민혁 트럭에 자두`
   (the place stays in the name). A later `태오가 민혁에게 자두를 네 개 빌려줬어` is kept, and the receiver's count
   is not said. The turn returns `{"act": "hold", "reason": "unread_event", "said": <the earlier statement>,
   "kept": true}`, so the reply says the earlier statement was not read, which is not true: it was read, under
   another holder. Asked: a reason (`other_key`, say) with `said` and the two keys, and a plan that says the
   count of the receiver may be the one that statement gave.

2. **A statement said with no amount, kept for a later amount** (`가람이 나래에게 연필을 줬어.`). The reader keeps
   it as an event whose verb it does not know (`unknown_word`), and the reply asks what `줬어` means. The verb is
   known; the amount is missing. An amount said in the next turn (`세 개.`) is now read into it. Asked: when the
   verb is a known verb of the frame, a plan that asks how many (the `vague_count` wording fits better than
   `unknown_word`). The reader would need to set a reason such as `missing_amount` at that site.

3. **The counter of the answer.** A count asked with `권`, `장`, `자루`, `송이` is answered with `개`
   (`보늬 님께 남은 공책은 몇 권이세요?` -> `6개입니다.`). The query carries the pack's `render` of the count
   question, which has `개입니다.`; the asked counter is in the question's text only. Asked: render the asked
   counter (the reader can add the counter word to the query meaning if the plan wants it as a field).
