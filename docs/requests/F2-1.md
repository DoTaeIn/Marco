# F2-1: a "more" or "total" answer is composed from one holder's count

From: goal F2 (reasoning gate), branch `reasoning-gate`. For: the owners of
`marco/language/realizer/` and the answered-result site of `marco/reasoning/context.py`.
Lines at `70ba9f8`. Found by the frozen reasoning set; nothing in F2 changes it.

## What happens

The engine answers both questions right; the realizer then says something else.

| Question (after `Omar has 9 stamps.` `Lena has 4 stamps.`) | engine sentence | spoken reply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Who has more stamps, Omar or Lena?` | `Omar.` | `4 stamps.` |
| `How many stamps do Omar and Lena have together?` | `13 stamps.` | `9 stamps.` |

Same in Korean (`태린과 누리 중 누가 단추가 더 많아?` → `4개입니다.`,
`두 사람 단추를 합치면 몇 개야?` → `9개입니다.`).

Why: the answered result (`marco/reasoning/context.py:3614-3617`) carries
`meaning: {"act": "inform", "query": null, "render": ...}` — a total or "more"
query has no `triple`, so `query` is null — and its transitions list each
member's count as a `fact` row. The declared plan "an answered question"
(`marco/language/realizer/meaning.json:83`, `match: status answered, source fact`)
takes the last `fact` row (`marco/language/realizer/meaning.py:141`,
`answered_fact`) as the answer and composes that holder's count. The semantic
check passes because the sentence does say a count that is in the facts.

## Effect on the gates

Reasoning baseline at `70ba9f8`: all 24 wrong questions are this (comparison 8,
total 12, correction 4 — the corrections are "more" questions). Gate condition 6
needs 0 wrong. The taller comparisons are not affected: their answer fact is
the derived `[winner, taller, loser]` row and the realizer has no plan for it,
so the engine's right sentence passes through (condition 5 counts it as not
composed).

## Request

Either of:

1. The answered result states what was answered in its `meaning`: for a total,
   `{"act": "inform", "total": {"members": [...], "item": ..., "value": n}}`; for
   "more", `{"act": "inform", "more": {"candidates": [a, b], "item": ..., "winner": a}}`
   (the same for `taller`), and the realizer declares plans that compose them.
2. Until then, the "answered question" plan does not match a result whose
   `meaning.query` is null, so the engine's sentence passes through (counted as
   not composed by gate condition 5, but not wrong).
