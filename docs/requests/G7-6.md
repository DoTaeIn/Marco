# G7-6: two replies about counts kept by their unit

From: G7-S (statements), branch `understanding-r7-statements`, commit 043b0da (one thing counted in two units).
For: the owner of `marco/language/realizer/` and the reply-return sites. The statements side changed no plan
and no reply site; both cases below return the closest existing meaning.

## What the recording does now

`노라는 깃펜이 43묶음 있어.` then `노라는 깃펜이 8개 있어.` are two counts of one thing in two declared units
(`ReasoningContext._unit_keys`): 43 under `노라 깃펜 묶음`, 8 under `노라 깃펜`. A count question that asks in one
of the units reads that count; any other count question about that holder and thing is held
(`ReasoningContext._asked_in_unit`).

## Asked

1. **The hold that should name the units.** A count question in no unit, or in a unit the conversation did not
   count in (`노라는 깃펜이 몇 자루 있어?`), returns

   ```
   {"act": "hold", "reason": "unresolved", "subject": "노라 깃펜",
    "units": [{"unit": "묶음", "key": "노라 깃펜 묶음"}, {"unit": "개", "key": "노라 깃펜"}]}
   ```

   and is said by the plain `unresolved` plan: *"이 대화에서 확인한 조건만으로는 답을 결정할 수 없습니다."* True, but
   it does not say why. Asked: a plan for `unresolved` with `units` that names the two units and their counts and
   asks in which the count is wanted, e.g. *"노라 깃펜은 43묶음과 8개로 따로 들었습니다. 어느 단위로 셀까요?"* (the
   counts are the state's values under each `key`). A reason of your naming is fine (`which_unit`); tell G7-S
   and it will be emitted.

2. **The counter of the answer** (still open from G6-4 item 3). `노라는 깃펜이 몇 묶음 있어?` is answered
   `43개입니다.`: the number is the bundle count, the counter said is the pack's first unit. With two units kept
   apart this now reads as the wrong unit. The asked unit is `ReasoningContext._unit_said(parser, text,
   after_asker=True)`; the unit of a kept count is on its facts (`fact["unit"]`). Asked: the answer said in the
   unit asked (`43묶음입니다.`).
