# G7-5: a Korean thing said with words before its noun is split into the holder

From: G7-Q (questions), branch `understanding-r7-questions`, step 3 of the identity graph.
For: G7-S (statements): the reader names each fact's holder and thing (statement side of step 3).

## What happens

```
제 친구 미경은 줄무늬 면 수건이 5개 있어.
제 친구 수아는 줄무늬 수건이 3개 있어.
```

The graph after these two statements, at effort 3:

| Node | Name |
| --- | --- |
| h1 | `미경 줄무늬 면` |
| h2 | `수아 줄무늬` |
| t1 | `수건` |

The replay keys are `미경 줄무늬 면 수건` and `수아 줄무늬 수건`. The words before the noun (`줄무늬 면`, `줄무늬`)
go to the holder, not the thing. The English form of the same pair (`My cousin Lena has 5 striped cotton towels.`
/ `My cousin Omar has 3 striped towels.`) gives h1 `Lena`, h2 `Omar`, t1 `striped cotton towels` with the short
alias `striped towels`.

## What it breaks on the question side

- `그 친구는 수건이 몇 개야?` asks which of `수아 줄무늬 수건` and `미경 줄무늬 면 수건`. The reply `수아요.`
  rewrites the question to `수아는 수건이 몇 개야?`. The lookup finds no holder node `수아`: the holder node is
  `수아 줄무늬`. The reply is held.
- `그 친구는 줄무늬 수건이 몇 개야?` also reads `줄무늬` as a word about the person, not the thing.

The question side reads both correctly once the holder node is `수아` and the thing node is `줄무늬 면 수건` (with
`줄무늬 수건` as its short alias). Some of the thing's words then name it (`ConversationGraph.part_of`, G7-Q step 3).

## Asked

When the reader names a count fact's holder and thing in Korean, the words between the holder (with its topic or
subject particle) and the counted noun belong to the thing: `미경` + `줄무늬 면 수건`. Two statements that say
the thing with fewer of its words then join as one thing node by the existing rule (`_thing_node`, form `short`).

Test to hold it: the two statements above, then `그 친구는 수건이 몇 개야?`, `수아요.` → answered, 3.
