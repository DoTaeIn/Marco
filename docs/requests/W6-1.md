`about.value` and `about.recorded` are integers. A `recorded` of 0 is said as having none.

holder `탐은`, so the denied clause fails the semantic check and the whole differing-number reply is
held ("답을 보류합니다."). Safe, never wrong; it composes once the reader keeps one-syllable names.
